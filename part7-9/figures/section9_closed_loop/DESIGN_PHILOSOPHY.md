# Measured Space

Geometry carries meaning before decoration. A shared coordinate origin, a
continuous trajectory, and a visible arrowhead establish relationships through
position and direction. Meticulously crafted alignment lets a reader follow a
system without reconstructing its geometry from accompanying prose.

White space belongs to the structure. It separates symbols from lines and gives
annotations a clear place beside the relationships they describe. Direct labels
use that space without sample strokes or a legend. Painstaking attention to
clearance preserves a quiet rhythm across repeated panels.

A small palette distinguishes mechanisms through restrained accents. Solid,
dashed, and outlined forms retain their meaning in grayscale. Icons replace
empty placeholders only when their silhouettes identify an action: a proof
document, a checked shield, a published advisory, or a toxic bottle. Each symbol
receives the same level of refinement as the surrounding scientific geometry.

Typography remains consistent and subordinate to the mechanism. Mathematical
subscripts and fractions are drawn as vector forms; short objective statements
sit in deliberate gaps. Expert craftsmanship appears in the restrained second
pass: endpoint alignment, readable small shapes, and careful spacing rather
than additional ornament. The conceptual thread is a visible return from
evidence to renewed inquiry.
